A large collection of data needed to move through software that lived in a browser. Ten terabytes is not a normal browser download, and treating it like one makes the limits of the usual approach obvious very quickly.

The work centered on how progress survived interruptions and which responsibilities belonged in the browser rather than on the machines behind it. Most of the design became clear only after the simple version stopped being simple.
